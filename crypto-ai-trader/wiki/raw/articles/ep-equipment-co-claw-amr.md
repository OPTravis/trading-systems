## Overview

The EP Equipment Co-Claw is an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) designed for collaborative material handling in warehouses and logistics facilities. The "Co-Claw" name suggests a collaborative ("co-") robot with a claw/gripper mechanism ("-claw"), indicating it's designed to work alongside human workers while autonomously handling loads.

## Key Features

- **Collaborative Design**: Built to operate safely alongside human workers
- **Autonomous Navigation**: SLAM-based navigation with obstacle avoidance
- **Load Handling**: Integrated claw/gripper mechanism for automated pick and transport
- **Compact Form Factor**: Suitable for indoor warehouse environments
- **Flexible Deployment**: Adaptable to various warehouse layouts and workflows
- **Human-robot Collaboration**: Designed to augment rather than replace human workers

## The "Co-Claw" Concept

The "Co-Claw" name encapsulates the product's design philosophy:

- **"Co-" (Collaborative)**: The robot is designed to work in shared spaces with human workers, using safety-rated sensors and speed limiting to ensure safe human-robot interaction
- **"Claw"**: Refers to the integrated gripper/end-effector mechanism that allows the robot to autonomously pick up, hold, and transport loads without human assistance

This positions the Co-Claw in the emerging "collaborative mobile robot" category, distinct from:
- Traditional AGVs (follow fixed paths, no manipulation)
- Industrial robots (stationary, caged for safety)
- Pure AMRs (mobile but no load manipulation capability)
