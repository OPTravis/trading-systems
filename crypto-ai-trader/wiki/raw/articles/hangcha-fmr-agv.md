## Overview

The Hangcha FMR (Forklift Mobile Robot) series is a line of heavy-duty automated guided vehicles built on an 80V electrical platform. The FMR AGVs are designed for autonomous pallet handling, stacking, and transport in large-scale warehouses, distribution centers, and manufacturing facilities. They combine the robustness of traditional Hangcha forklift engineering with autonomous navigation and fleet management capabilities.

## Key Features

- **Heavy-duty Capability**: Built for high-capacity pallet handling tasks
- **80V Platform**: Higher voltage electrical system for improved power efficiency, longer duty cycles, and greater lifting capacity compared to 48V/24V systems
- **Fork-based Design**: Traditional forklift mast with autonomous fork positioning
- **Multi-sensor Navigation**: LiDAR, camera, and safety sensor suite for reliable autonomous operation
- **Industrial-grade Build**: Designed for 24/7 operation in demanding industrial environments
- **Fleet Orchestration**: Integrates with Hangcha's intelligent logistics management platform

## 80V Platform Advantages

The 80V electrical platform is a significant differentiator:

| Feature | 48V System | 80V System |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Power efficiency | Standard | Higher |
| Duty cycle | Good | Extended |
| Heat generation | Higher | Lower |
| Cable gauge | Thicker needed | Thinner possible |
| Lifting capacity | Moderate | Higher |
| Best for | Light-medium duty | Heavy-duty, 24/7 |

The 80V platform allows the FMR AGVs to handle heavier loads with better energy efficiency, making them suitable for high-throughput, multi-shift operations where downtime for charging is costly.
