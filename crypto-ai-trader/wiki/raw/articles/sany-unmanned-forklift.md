## Overview

SANY Robotics, a subsidiary of the SANY Group (one of China's largest heavy machinery manufacturers), develops unmanned forklift solutions for warehouse and logistics automation. The SANY unmanned forklift leverages the parent company's expertise in heavy equipment engineering to create robust autonomous material handling vehicles.

## Key Features

- **Industrial-grade Construction**: Built to SANY's heavy machinery standards
- **Autonomous Navigation**: LiDAR + vision-based SLAM navigation
- **Heavy-duty Capability**: Designed for demanding industrial environments
- **24/7 Operation**: Continuous operation with automated charging
- **Fleet Management**: Multi-vehicle coordination and WMS integration
- **Safety Systems**: Comprehensive obstacle detection and emergency stop

## SANY Robotics Division

SANY Robotics was established to commercialize autonomous mobile robot technology, drawing on SANY Group's decades of experience in:

- **Heavy equipment design**: Structural engineering, hydraulics, powertrain
- **Global manufacturing**: Factories across China, India, USA, Europe, Brazil
- **R&D capabilities**: SANY invests heavily in automation and digital transformation
- **Industrial IoT**: SANY's "tree-root" industrial internet platform

The robotics division focuses on:
1. **Unmanned forklifts**: Autonomous counterbalance and warehouse forklifts
2. **AGV/AMR platforms**: Various autonomous mobile robot form factors
3. **Intelligent logistics systems**: End-to-end warehouse automation solutions

## Strategic Context

SANY Group is best known as the world's largest concrete machinery manufacturer and one of China's top heavy equipment makers (alongside XCMG, Zoomlion, and LiuGong). The move into autonomous forklifts and warehouse robotics represents:

1. **Technology diversification**: Leveraging autonomous driving technology developed for construction equipment (autonomous mining trucks, unmanned excavators)
2. **Market expansion**: Entering the high-growth warehouse automation market
3. **Synergy**: Applying heavy equipment engineering expertise to material handling
4. **Policy alignment**: Supporting China's "Made in China 2025" and "Robot+" initiatives
