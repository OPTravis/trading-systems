# 杭叉 EZ-GO AGV

## Source Reference

- **Product**: EZ-GO AGV

- **Series**: Hangcha Intelligent Logistics Equipment Line

## Overview

The Hangcha EZ-GO is a compact automated guided vehicle (AGV) designed for indoor material transport in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers. As part of Hangcha's intelligent logistics product portfolio, the EZ-GO is positioned as an accessible, easy-to-deploy solution for automating routine horizontal transport tasks.

## Key Features

- **Compact Design**: Small footprint suitable for narrow warehouse aisles and congested environments
- **Ease of Deployment**: Designed for rapid setup with minimal infrastructure modification
- **Navigation**: Multi-sensor navigation (likely SLAM-based with LiDAR, camera, and/or magnetic tape options)
- **Load Transport**: Flat-top or roller configuration for cart/tote/pallet transport
- **Fleet Management**: Compatible with Hangcha's intelligent fleet management system for multi-vehicle coordination

## Use Cases

1. **Tote/Cart Transport**: Moving totes, bins, or carts between picking stations and packing areas
2. **Line Feeding**: Delivering components to production lines in manufacturing
3. **Goods-to-Person**: Transporting goods to stationary pickers in e-commerce fulfillment
4. **Cross-docking**: Moving goods between inbound and outbound dock areas

## Strategic Context

The EZ-GO represents Hangcha's push into the mid-market AGV segment, targeting small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) that need automation but may not have the budget or space for large-scale AGV deployments. The "EZ" branding emphasizes ease of use and low complexity, contrasting with Hangcha's heavy-duty FMR AGV line.

Hangcha's AGV portfolio spans:
- **EZ-GO**: Entry-level, compact AGV (this product)
- **FMR Series**: Heavy-duty AGV for pallet handling (see hangcha-fmr-agv.md)
- **CPD Series**: Autonomous counterbalance forklift AGVs

## Market Positioning

The compact AGV market in China is highly competitive, with players including:
- **Quicktron (快仓)**: FlashPick series
- **Hikvision Robotics (海康机器人)**: Q-series AMR
- **Geek+ (极智杰)**: P-series picking robots
- **Hangcha EZ-GO**: Positioned as a reliable, forklift-manufacturer-backed alternative

Hangcha's advantage lies in its established dealer/service network and reputation in material handling equipment, providing customers confidence in after-sales support.
