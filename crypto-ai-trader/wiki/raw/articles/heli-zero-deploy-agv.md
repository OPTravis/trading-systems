## Overview

The 合小力 (HeLi Xiao Li) is a zero-deploy autonomous mobile robot (AMR/AGV) developed by Anhui Heli, China's largest forklift manufacturer and the world's #7 forklift maker by revenue. The "zero-deploy" concept refers to the robot's ability to operate without requiring pre-installed infrastructure such as magnetic tape, reflectors, or predefined paths — it can be deployed with minimal setup time and cost.

## Key Features

- **Zero-deploy Philosophy**: No infrastructure modifications needed — no magnetic tape, no reflectors, no fixed paths
- **Rapid Deployment**: Operational within hours rather than days/weeks
- **Natural Navigation**: SLAM-based (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) using LiDAR and sensors
- **Compact Form Factor**: Designed for indoor warehouse environments
- **User-friendly Interface**: Simplified setup and operation, accessible to non-technical users
- **Scalable**: Can start with a single unit and scale to multi-robot fleets

## The "Zero-deploy" Concept

Traditional AGV deployment requires:
1. Infrastructure survey and planning
2. Installation of navigation aids (magnetic tape, reflectors, QR codes)
3. Path programming and testing
4. Safety system validation
5. Operator training

The 合小力 eliminates or minimizes steps 1-4 through:
- **Self-mapping**: The robot maps its environment autonomously using onboard sensors
- **Dynamic path planning**: Calculates optimal routes in real-time
- **No fixed infrastructure**: Operates using natural features of the environment for navigation
- **Quick start**: From unboxing to productive operation in minimal time

This "zero-deploy" approach significantly reduces the total cost of ownership (TCO) and lowers the barrier to automation adoption, particularly for:
- Small and medium warehouses
- First-time automation users
- Facilities with frequently changing layouts
- Temporary or seasonal operations

## Strategic Context

Anhui Heli is the dominant forklift manufacturer in China with approximately 25-30% domestic market share. The company has been expanding into intelligent logistics equipment as part of its "intelligent manufacturing" strategy:

1. **Traditional forklifts**: Core business, comprehensive range from 1-ton to 46-ton capacity
2. **Electric forklifts**: Growing segment, lithium-ion powered
3. **AGV/AMR products**: 合小力 and other autonomous vehicle lines
4. **Intelligent logistics solutions**: Integrated warehouse automation

The 合小力 brand name uses a personification strategy — making the robot feel like a friendly, helpful assistant rather than a complex industrial machine. This aligns with the "zero-deploy" easy-to-use positioning.
